This page explains the everyday moves in an application built with django-generic: finding your way, finding a record, filtering a list, opening a record's page, adding, changing or deleting a record, and following what changes. Every screen of the application works the same way: what you learn on one list holds for all the others.

The names of the menus and of the data depend on your application; the examples below speak of "tickets" or "customers" to fix ideas. What you see also depends on your permissions: a missing button is usually an action that is not open to you.

1. Finding your way

Signing in

The application opens on the sign-in page: your username and password, or your organisation's sign-in button when one is offered. After several failures the account is locked for a few minutes. To sign out: the account menu (your initials, top right), then Log out.

The frame of every page

  • The navigation, on the left, lists the screens you may open, in groups. A click on a group's title folds or unfolds it, and the choice is remembered. The Filter navigation box at the very top finds a screen by its name.
  • The top bar holds the button that opens or closes the navigation, the pin that keeps it beside the page (or lets it float: it then slides out of the left edge of the screen when the pointer reaches it), the application's name (back to the dashboard) and the breadcrumbs, which say where you are and take you up a level.
  • On the right: the search, the notification bell, the theme button (light, dark or the system's) and the account menu.

On a telephone or a small window, the navigation opens over the page with the same button.

2. The dashboard

It is the home page. Depending on what the application provides, it shows:

  • wiki pages pinned to it: announcements, instructions, how-tos;
  • shortcuts: cards leading straight to an already filtered list, a form or another site, sometimes with a count;
  • key figures: a click on a figure opens the list of the records it counts, filtered the same way;
  • record cards: the most urgent, the latest; See all opens the matching list.

They refresh by themselves when the data changes.

3. Finding anything: the search

The Search… box of the top bar, or Ctrl+K (or the / key), opens the search palette. Type a few letters: it offers the screens, the records (by name or reference) and the wiki pages that match, grouped by kind. The arrows move through the results, Enter opens the chosen one, Escape closes the palette.

4. Lists

Each kind of record has its list: a table, with a search box and a toolbar above it (Filter, Views, Columns, Export, Reload the rows), and sometimes charts. The page's own buttons (Add, Import, Watch every record, a calendar…) are at the top right.

Reading and sorting

  • A click on a column's header sorts the table on it; a second click reverses the order.
  • A name in the link colour is a link: it opens that record (a customer, an assignee…).
  • Coloured values (a status, a priority, tags) are tags: they filter like any other value.
  • Under the table: the number of rows, how many rows a page shows, and the pages.

Searching a list

The Search or filter… box searches every column meant for searching. Every word must be there: invoice blank finds the rows holding both words. A ! before a word excludes it (invoice !blank), quotes search for a phrase as written ("password reset").

Filtering

There are five ways to set a filter, all leading to the same result:

  1. the Filter button (or the F key): pick a column, then the condition;
  2. the funnel appearing in a column's header when the pointer is on it;
  3. the search row under the headers: a field per column (the button beside Filter shows or hides it);
  4. a typed filter in the search box, shaped column:value, such as status:open,pending; suggestions appear as you type;
  5. a right click on a cell: Filter on this value, or leave it out.

The filter editor fits the column: a list of values to tick with how many rows hold each (statuses, relations, tags), "contains / starts with / is" for a text, "greater than / between" for a number, "today / this week / the last 30 days / between two dates" for a date, "yes / no" for a checkbox, and everywhere "is empty / is not empty". The filter applies while you change it; Done closes the editor, Cancel puts things back as they were.

The filters in force are chips under the toolbar: a click on a chip changes it, its cross removes it, Clear all removes them all. With several filters, the start of the bar chooses whether rows must match all of them or any of them; Add a group combines both (such as "open AND (urgent OR opened more than 30 days ago)").

In the search row and in typed filters, a few short forms are understood:

  • text: =Invoice the exact value, ^SD-10 a start, a,b either;
  • number: 5, >10, <=2.5, 2..8 (between 2 and 8);
  • date: 2026-01-01, >=2026-01-01, 2026-01-01..2026-03-31, 30d (the last 30 days), +7d (the next 7), this-month;
  • everywhere: empty for rows without a value, and ! before to exclude (!empty).

The page's address keeps the filters and the search: a filtered list can be bookmarked, sent to a colleague or found again with the browser's Back button.

Charts above a list

They follow the table's filters and search. A click on a bar or a slice filters the table on that value. Their buttons show the figures as a table or download the chart as an image; some change their period (day, week, month…).

Views: a layout found again

The Views menu offers the views the application suggests and your views. Save this view keeps the current layout (columns, their order, sorting, filters, search, rows per page) under a name; a view can become the one the table opens with, and it follows you on every device. Back to the default layout undoes everything that was changed. Where the application allows it, Send by e-mail on a schedule mails the list regularly.

Choosing the columns

The Columns menu ticks the columns shown, with a box to find one by its name; Show all and Default are at its foot.

Exporting

The Export menu offers Excel and CSV: the file holds every row matching the filters and the search, not only the page on screen, with the visible columns in their order. Copy to the clipboard and Print take the page on screen only.

Acting on several rows

  1. Tick the rows. The header's box ticks the whole page; a link then offers to Select all N matching rows, every row under the current filters.
  2. A bar appears: Choose an action, then Apply. Dangerous actions ask first.
  3. Clear selection unticks everything.

A row's menu

The ⋯ button at the end of a row, or a right click on it, opens its menu: Open, Edit, Delete and the other actions you may run, with filters on the value clicked. A double click on a row opens the record.

Editing in a table

Some tables are grids: every cell that may be changed is already a field. A list, a switch or a date saves at once; a text saves with Enter (the cursor moves down a row, as in a spreadsheet) or when you leave the cell; Escape cancels the typing. A saved cell flashes green; an error shows under the cell. Add a row adds a row at the top of the table, sent with ✓ or Enter, discarded with ✕.

5. A record's page

A click on a record opens its page (its summary):

  • at the top, its name, the Watch and Edit buttons, the ⋯ menu (More actions) and the actions of this kind of record (changing a status, for instance);
  • a line says who changed it last, and when, with a See the history link;
  • figures as tiles and, sometimes, charts;
  • its values, in sections that fold; a related record is a link to its own page, an attached file downloads with a click;
  • tabs, one per kind of related record (a customer's tickets, a ticket's time entries…), with their count. Each tab is a whole list: search, filters, views, export, actions. Its Add button creates a record already attached to this one. When the tabs do not fit, the last ones go behind a +N button.

The page refreshes by itself when somebody changes the record while you are looking at it.

6. Adding, changing, deleting

Adding

The Add button at the top of a list, a shortcut on the dashboard, or Add in a tab of a record's page open an empty form (in the last case, already attached to the record). In a calendar, a day's + opens the form with that date.

Filling in a form

  • An asterisk * marks a required field. Fields are grouped in sections, sometimes folded or in tabs.
  • A list of choices is searched by typing a few letters; its cross empties it. Beside a list naming another record, the pencil edits the chosen one and the + creates a new one, in a small window: it is chosen as soon as it is saved, and nothing typed so far is lost.
  • A file: Choose a file, or Replace and Remove for a file already there. The change happens when you save.
  • Rows inside the form (comments, steps…) are filled in right there: Add another… adds a row, Remove takes one away (and Undo puts it back).

Saving

At the foot of the form: Save (back to the record's page), Save and continue editing (stay on the form), Save and add another (an empty form opens) and Cancel. Ctrl+S saves too. An error shows under its field, and nothing is saved until it is fixed. Leaving the page with unsaved changes asks first.

Changing

The Edit button of a record's page, or Edit in a row's menu, opens the same form, filled in. Its tabs also lead to the history; Summary goes back to the record's page.

Deleting

Delete is at the foot of the form, in a row's menu, or among the bulk actions. Before deleting, a window shows everything that goes with the record (its comments, its lines…); Yes, delete confirms. When protected records depend on it, the deletion is refused and the window names them. When the screen has a trash, the record is only moved there (Move to the trash): the list's Trash page can Restore it.

7. History

Every change is kept. The History tab of a record's page (or the See the history link) lists its versions, newest first: who, when, and each value before → after. Show this version in full shows the record as it was then. The Changes page, when it is open to you, lists every change of the whole application, with the usual filters and exports.

8. Importing from a spreadsheet

When a list offers Import:

  1. choose or drop an Excel or CSV file; Download a template gives an empty workbook with the right headers;
  2. match each column of the file to a field;
  3. check the Preview: what will be created, updated, left unchanged, and the errors row by row;
  4. Import writes everything, or nothing while a row is in error.

9. Other ways of looking at the data

Calendar

Some dated records have a calendar (a button at the top of their list, or an entry in the navigation): Month, Week or List, the arrows to change the period, Today to come back. The list's search and filters choose what it shows. A record is dragged onto another day to change its date, when you may change it; a click opens it.

Tree

Records holding other records (a bill of materials, a hierarchy) show as a tree: a row's arrow unfolds it, and the number beside the name says how many it holds. With the keyboard: arrows up and down, right to unfold, left to fold, Enter to open. Collapse all and Refresh are above it; the search looks at every level.

10. Staying informed

  • Watch: on a record's page, this button has you told when the record changes; the arrow beside it also offers to watch every record of its kind. On a list, Watch every record does the same.
  • Watching, in the account menu, lists what you watch and sets, for each, which changes matter and how you hear about them.
  • The bell shows how many notifications are unread; its menu shows the latest, Mark all as read and See all notifications. A notification also shows as a message on the open page, and by e-mail if you chose it.

11. The wiki

The wiki holds what the team writes down. Wiki, in the navigation, lists the wikis; Open goes into one, PDF downloads the whole of it (portrait or landscape).

  • On the left, the menu of its pages, as a tree, with a box to filter them; the other wikis unfold under its name.
  • Edit opens the editor: headings, bold, italics, lists, quotes, code, links, images and attached files (from the toolbar, dropped or pasted). The toolbar's arrows, or Alt+Up and Alt+Down, move the current line. The editor also sets the parent page, the position in the menu and whether the page is pinned to the dashboard. Save or Cancel.
  • Subpage creates a page under this one; the menu's + creates a page.
  • History lists the earlier versions; Restore brings one back (the current one is kept, so it can be undone).
  • If somebody saved the page while you were editing it, your save is refused with their name, and your text stays in the editor to be copied.

12. Your account

The account menu (your initials) leads to the Account settings, Notifications, Watching, Help and What changed (the changes of each version); it changes the language and signs you out. The Account settings page holds:

  • Profile: first name, last name, e-mail address;
  • Appearance: the System, Light or Dark theme, ready-made palettes (Soft, High contrast…) and sliders for the colour, the contrast, the striped rows;
  • Preferences: language, navigation pinned or floating, rows per page, how you are notified (in the application, by e-mail or both), and Remember table layouts to find each list as you left it;
  • Security: Change password.

These settings are kept on your account: they follow you from one computer to the next. The size of the text follows the browser's (Ctrl and + or −).

13. Keyboard shortcuts

  • Ctrl+K or /: the search;
  • F: filter the list on screen;
  • Ctrl+S: save a form;
  • Escape: close a menu, a window, the palette;
  • Enter: confirm a cell, open the chosen result;
  • Alt+Up / Alt+Down: move a line in the wiki's editor;
  • Shift + right click: the browser's own menu in a table.

14. When something is wrong

  • A button or a screen is missing: it is not open to your account; ask an administrator.
  • A list looks empty: look at the filter chips and the search box, or use Back to the default layout in the Views menu.
  • A page looks outdated after the application was updated: reload it with Ctrl+Shift+R.
  • A restart is announced by a banner: save your work before the time it gives.
